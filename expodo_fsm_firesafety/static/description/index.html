Built for fire safety companies

A screen that speaks the trade. Open a service order, log the device, its agent and location, pick the job type, work through the steps and invoice. Extinguisher, alarm, detector, emergency light or hose reel, each job carries the equipment and the building it protects, not a generic service form bent into shape.

Inspection and testing, built into the job

Every order carries the last inspection, the next due date, the last and next hydrostatic pressure test, the inspection body and the certificate reference, so a due date never slips. A condition block records the pressure gauge, the tamper seal, the safety pin, the hose and nozzle, the access and the signage, right on the job rather than on a separate sheet.

See it in action

Every job at a glance

The kanban board lines up each device by stage, from To Plan through Planned, In Progress and To Invoice. Brand, equipment type, job type and the technician in charge all show right on the card.

The full device and inspection record

Type, agent, brand, serial and location sit on the order, with capacity, then the last and next inspection and pressure test, the body and certificate, and a condition block below. Everything about the device and the job on one screen.

A checklist for every job type

Each job type carries its own steps, ready to use. A periodic inspection opens with check the pressure gauge and tamper seal, check the safety pin, hose and nozzle, verify access and signage, and record findings and tag the device. The technician ticks as they go and nothing slips through.

From service order to invoice

The order list shows the customer, the brand, the next inspection due, the job and where it stands on billing. One click on Create Invoice turns the labour and parts into an Odoo accounting entry.
